Memento · a voice journal for Windows

Speak your thoughts. Memento writes them down.

For people who think better out loud. It runs on your own PC: no paid AI plan, no subscription.

The download is not available at the moment.

Version 1.3.0 Pilot Windows 10 or 11 · NVIDIA RTX graphics card

Say it. See it. Keep it.

  1. 1

    Your words appear as you speak

    Each thing you say is written on the screen while you talk, pauses included.

  2. 2

    Fillers go. Your wording stays.

    Here is the cleaned text: Memento highlights one part so you can check its punctuation before saving.

  3. 3

    Tags settle on the entry

    Every entry keeps its tags, and a tag filter brings your entries back in a click.

A journal that stays yours

  • Plain text files, in your folder

    Each sitting becomes a dated text file in a folder you choose, readable without Memento.

  • English and French, your pick

    Use Memento in English or French, and give each journal folder its own language.

  • Pick up where you left off

    If Memento crashes in the middle of a sitting, it offers the unfinished sitting back at the next start.

Will it run on your PC?

What you need

  • Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit
  • An NVIDIA graphics card, RTX 20 series or newer, with at least 6 GB of graphics memory
  • Internet for the first start only: a one-time download of about 5.1 GB
Show allShow less
  • An NVIDIA driver, version 580 or newer
  • A microphone
  • A little over 7 GB of free disk space
  • 16 GB of memory should be enough, though only 32 GB has been measured

What it does not do

  • It does not work without a supported NVIDIA card: on other PCs you can read your journal, but not record
  • It understands only English and French: speech in a language other than your journal’s comes out as an automatic translation, not your words
  • It does not rewrite your text, summarise it or give advice
Show allShow less
  • It cannot search the text of entries, or edit or delete them in the app; you can filter by tag
  • It does not back up your journal or update itself
  • It does not run on phones or Macs
  • It is not signed: Windows shows a warning during setup, and an antivirus program may wrongly flag it

Pilot.Memento 1.3.0 is a pilot for friends and enthusiasts. It is free. A full version, with a licence to buy, is in the making.

What Windows may show

This installer is not signed, so Windows may stop it and show a blue window: “Windows protected your PC”. To go on, choose “More info”, then “Run anyway”.

On some Windows 11 PCs, Smart App Control may refuse to run the installer, with no way to continue. If that happens, please tell us: Report a problem

You use Memento at your own risk. If you would rather not download it, that is fine.
